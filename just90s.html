Live internet radio, dedicated exclusively to 90s music stations from around the world. No filler decades, no genre soup, just the 90s, 24/7, in a Classic Media Player interface built to match.

Built like the decade
it celebrates.

100% Nineties
Every station is filtered for genuine 90s music, nothing else sneaks in.
Classic Player UI
A Winamp-inspired interface with a live LED equalizer and VU meters, driven by real audio analysis.
