Privacy Policy

Last updated — February 2025

01

Introduction

Innovation Hub Studio Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our RadioLive mobile application.

02

Information We Collect

Our app is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary to provide you with the best radio streaming experience:

  • Device information — Basic device data for app optimisation
  • Usage data — Information about how you use the app
  • Network information — To ensure optimal streaming quality
03

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our radio streaming service
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Analyse usage patterns to enhance features
  • Ensure technical functionality and prevent abuse
04

Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or streaming. These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

05

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

06

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of certain data collection practices
07

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Questions

Get in touch

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@innovationhubstudio.co.uk
