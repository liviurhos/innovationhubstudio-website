2. What the service is, and what it is not

Collision Radar searches publicly available app store data and shows you applications whose public descriptions are similar to an idea you describe. It reports raw comparison data: names, developers, categories, public store links, and a computed similarity score.

Collision Radar does not predict, guarantee, or influence app store review outcomes. Apple and Google review every submission manually, using criteria that are their own and that change over time. A scan showing few similar apps is not a promise of approval. A scan showing many is not a verdict of rejection. The data is provided to inform your own judgement, and any decision to build, submit, or abandon an idea is yours alone.

Similarity scores are produced by automated language analysis of public descriptions. They are approximate by nature and may include apps that are not true competitors, or miss apps that are.
