App for iOS and Android

Collision
Radar

Describe your app idea. Collision Radar searches the App Store and Google Play for the most similar apps already published, ranked by semantic similarity, so you know the landscape before you build. Raw comparison data, not verdicts. Your ideas are never stored.

  • 01 Semantic search Goes beyond keywords. Multilingual language analysis compares the meaning of app descriptions, finding functional overlap even when the wording differs.
  • 02 Both stores One scan covers the App Store or Google Play, your choice, with the top 30 most similar apps and a minimum estimate of the market's size.
  • 03 Private by design Ideas are processed in memory and discarded. No accounts, no tracking, no ads. An anonymous identifier is the only thing the app knows about you.
  • 04 Simple pricing 4 free scans on every device, then straightforward credit packs. No subscriptions, no accounts to manage.
